# KB-005 — Payment Failure Handling

**Article ID:** KB-005  
**Category:** Billing  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 2023-11-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-08-10

## Summary

This article covers the process for handling failed or rejected payments, including auto-debit failures, card declines, UPI failures, and net banking issues.

## Common Payment Failure Reasons

| Failure Code | Description |
|---|---|
| INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS | Account balance insufficient |
| CARD_EXPIRED | Card has expired |
| CARD_DECLINED | Bank declined the card (various reasons) |
| UPI_TIMEOUT | UPI transaction timed out |
| BANK_SERVER_ERROR | Bank's system unavailable |
| INVALID_ACCOUNT | Account number entered incorrectly |
| FRAUD_BLOCK | Bank blocked due to suspected fraud |

## Step 1 — Identify the Failure

1. Check the payment log for the transaction ID and failure code.
2. Confirm whether the payment amount was debited (partial debit) or not debited at all.

**CRITICAL:** If payment shows as "debited" at the customer's bank but "failed" on the system, this is a **pending reconciliation issue**, not a regular payment failure. Do NOT advise the customer that their payment failed — the money may be in transit. Escalate immediately.

## Step 2 — Advise the Customer

Based on failure code:

- **INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS:** Ask customer to add funds and retry.
- **CARD_EXPIRED:** Ask customer to update payment method.
- **UPI_TIMEOUT:** Safe to retry immediately. Usually resolves within 1-3 attempts.
- **BANK_SERVER_ERROR:** Advise retry after 30 minutes.
- **FRAUD_BLOCK:** Customer must contact their bank to lift the block.

## Step 3 — Service Suspension Warning

Unpaid invoices trigger service warnings:
- **Day 1-3 after due date:** Reminder SMS/email.
- **Day 4-7 after due date:** Final notice.
- **Day 8+:** Service may be restricted (browsing-only for broadband; incoming-only for mobile).
- **Day 15+:** Full suspension.
- **Day 30+:** Account termination process begins.

**NOTE:** Agents cannot override the automated suspension timeline without supervisor approval.

## Step 4 — Grace Period

First-time missed payment (account has clean 12-month history):
- A 7-day grace period extension can be offered.
- Only one grace period per 12 months.
- Must be logged with reason.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate if:
- Payment was debited from customer but not credited to account (reconciliation issue).
- Customer reports multiple consecutive payment failures despite using different methods.
- Account shows payment-related fraud flag.
- Customer is requesting a grace period and has already used one in the last 12 months.
- Account is at risk of termination and payment dispute is unresolved.

**IMPORTANT:** Never disclose exact fraud flag details to the customer. Simply state that additional verification is required and escalate.
