# KB-013 — Data Pack and Speed Boost Add-ons

**Article ID:** KB-013  
**Category:** Plans  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Effective Date:** 2024-03-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-08-30

## Summary

This article covers data add-ons, speed boosts, and temporary upgrades available to mobile and broadband customers.

## Available Add-ons

### Mobile Data Packs

| Pack | Data | Validity | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mini Pack | 1 GB | 1 day | ₹25 |
| Daily Pack | 2 GB | 1 day | ₹45 |
| Weekly Pack | 10 GB | 7 days | ₹150 |
| Monthly Extra | 30 GB | 30 days | ₹299 |

Data packs are consumed after the base plan's data is exhausted.

### Broadband Speed Boost

Available for FiberMax plans only:
- Speed boost is a temporary 24-hour boost to maximum available speed.
- **NOTE:** Speed boost cannot be applied if the current plan already delivers the maximum provisioned speed on the infrastructure in the customer's area. In that case, a plan upgrade is required.
- Price: ₹99 for 24 hours.
- Customers can purchase a maximum of 3 speed boosts per billing cycle.

### Family Add-ons

For Family Connect plan holders:
- Up to 4 additional SIM connections at ₹199/month each.
- Each additional SIM shares the primary plan's data pool.

## Important Edge Cases

**EDGE CASE:** A customer may purchase a data pack but not see any increase in available data. This can happen if:
1. The data pack expired before all data was consumed (some packs have time limits).
2. The data pack was applied to the wrong account or number.
3. System processing delay (up to 15 minutes).

If after 15 minutes the data pack is not credited: Verify the transaction, and if confirmed, escalate to billing for manual credit application.

**EDGE CASE:** Speed boost on FiberMax 500 plan — some areas have infrastructure limited to 400 Mbps. Boosting beyond infrastructure capacity has no effect. Before selling a speed boost, verify the customer's area's infrastructure limit.

## Refund Policy for Add-ons

- Data packs: **No refund** once purchased (data may already be consumed).
- Speed boosts: Refund if the service was not delivered (technical failure only).
- Family SIM add-ons: Prorated on removal.

## Escalation Criteria

- Data pack purchased but not credited after 15 minutes.
- Speed boost purchased but speed did not increase (verify infrastructure limits first).
- Customer disputes an add-on they did not authorize.
