# KB-008 — Service Outage Handling

**Article ID:** KB-008  
**Category:** Technical Support  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-02-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-08-25

## Summary

Protocol for identifying, communicating, and resolving service outages affecting multiple customers in the same area or on the same network segment.

## Outage Classification

| Type | Description | ETA Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Planned Maintenance | Scheduled downtime with prior notice | Yes — committed window |
| Unplanned Minor | Affecting <100 customers | Best effort 2 hours |
| Unplanned Major | Affecting 100-10,000 customers | Best effort 4 hours |
| Critical | Affecting >10,000 customers / critical infrastructure | Executive priority |

## Step 1 — Check the Outage Dashboard

Before beginning any troubleshooting, **always check the outage dashboard** for the customer's area code and circuit ID.

- If a known outage exists for the area: **Do not troubleshoot.** Acknowledge the outage, give ETA if available.
- If no outage is listed: Proceed with troubleshooting (KB-002 for broadband, KB-003 for mobile).

## Step 2 — Opening an Outage Report

If a customer reports an issue and no outage is on record, but 5 or more tickets from the same area are received within 2 hours, a network operations ticket must be opened.

## Step 3 — Customer Communication During Outage

1. Acknowledge the inconvenience.
2. Do not speculate on causes (do not say "a cable was cut" unless confirmed).
3. Provide ETA if available. If not available: "Our network team is actively investigating. We'll update within 2 hours."
4. Offer to register the customer for SMS updates when the outage is resolved.
5. **Do NOT offer proactive bill credit** during the outage — this requires confirmation of outage duration. Credits are applied after resolution per KB-001.

## Step 4 — Post-Outage Actions

After the outage is resolved:
1. Verify the customer's service is restored.
2. If the outage exceeded 4 hours, advise the customer they may be eligible for a billing credit per KB-001.
3. Close the ticket with outage reference number.

## Critical: Conflicting Outage Records

**EDGE CASE:** The outage dashboard may lag behind actual network conditions by up to 30 minutes. If a customer insists service is down but no outage is showing:
1. Do not dismiss the customer.
2. Proceed to basic troubleshooting.
3. If troubleshooting shows a healthy connection on our side but the customer still experiences the issue: Escalate to NOC (Network Operations Center).

## Escalation Criteria

- Outage exceeds the committed ETA.
- Customer is a business account experiencing revenue impact.
- SLA applies (business customers may have uptime SLAs).
- ETA was missed and customer demands escalation.
- Physical infrastructure damage (cut cables, damaged nodes).
