# KB-003 — Mobile Network Troubleshooting

**Article ID:** KB-003  
**Category:** Mobile  
**Version:** 2.8  
**Effective Date:** 2023-09-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-07-20

## Summary

Troubleshooting guide for mobile network issues including no signal, dropped calls, mobile data not working, and SMS failures.

## Step 1 — Verify Network Coverage

1. Check network coverage map for the customer's location.
2. Check for active maintenance or tower outages in the area.
3. If indoor: Note that signal strength varies significantly between floors and rooms.

**If confirmed outage or maintenance:** Acknowledge, provide ETA if available.

## Step 2 — Basic Fixes

Ask the customer to try in order:

1. **Toggle Airplane Mode:** On for 15 seconds, then off. This forces the device to re-register on the network.
2. **Restart the device** completely (not just lock/unlock).
3. **Check SIM card:** Remove and reinsert SIM. Inspect for damage or dirt.
4. **Check network mode settings:** Device should be set to Auto (4G/5G/3G auto-select), not locked to a specific band.

**IMPORTANT:** If the customer has already tried these steps, do NOT ask them to repeat. Record what was already attempted.

## Step 3 — Mobile Data Specific

If calls work but mobile data does not:

1. Verify data is enabled on the device (Settings → Mobile Data → ON).
2. Check data remaining in the current billing cycle. Zero data remaining = no mobile data.
3. Verify APN settings match the network's required APN:
   - APN: `resolveiq.net`
   - Authentication: PAP or CHAP
   - MCC/MNC: as per SIM
4. If APN is correct and data still fails: reset network settings (note: this erases Wi-Fi passwords).

## Step 4 — International Roaming Data

If the customer is abroad and data is not working:
- Verify roaming is enabled on the account (check account flags).
- Verify the customer's plan includes international roaming.
- Note: Roaming may take up to 30 minutes to activate after arriving in a new country.
- See KB-011 — Roaming Support.

## Step 5 — SIM-Related Issues

If signal is completely absent (no bars at all, even outdoors in coverage area):

1. Try the SIM in another compatible device. If it works, the original device is the issue.
2. If SIM doesn't work in any device, the SIM may be faulty, deactivated, or blacklisted.
3. **Deactivated SIM:** May occur if account is suspended due to non-payment.
4. **Blacklisted SIM:** May occur due to fraud flag — requires manual review.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to Tier-2 if:
- Customer has no signal in normal coverage area after SIM reinsertion and device restart.
- Issue affects multiple devices on the same account simultaneously.
- SIM appears to be deactivated and account shows no payment issue.
- Customer reports calls connecting but voice is inaudible (network quality issue).
- Customer has already performed all basic troubleshooting steps without improvement.
- Issue recurs more than 3 times in 30 days.
