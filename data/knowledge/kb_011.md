# KB-011 — Roaming Support

**Article ID:** KB-011  
**Category:** Mobile  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective Date:** 2023-07-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-06-20

## Summary

Support guide for international roaming issues including activation, costs, data abroad, and country-specific limitations.

## Roaming Eligibility

Roaming is available on:
- Mobile Plus, Mobile Max, Family Connect, Unlimited Pro plans.
- **NOT available on:** Mobile Lite plan (no international roaming).

To enable roaming:
1. Verify plan includes roaming.
2. Enable roaming flag on the account.
3. Inform customer roaming activation may take up to 2 hours.
4. Advise customer to toggle Airplane mode off/on in the destination country.

## Roaming Rates

**IMPORTANT NOTE:** Roaming rates vary by destination country. We do not provide rate quotes in support conversations — rates are available on the website plan details page. Advise customers to check rates before traveling.

**Key rate zones:**
- Zone A (neighboring countries): Standard daily pass rates.
- Zone B (major global destinations): Premium daily pass rates.
- Zone C (others): Pay-per-use rates (significantly higher).

The specific countries in each zone are listed on the website and are updated periodically. Do NOT quote specific rates — they change frequently.

## Common Roaming Problems

### Problem: Data not working abroad

1. Verify roaming is enabled on account.
2. Verify plan includes roaming.
3. Verify device roaming setting is ON.
4. Allow 30 minutes after arrival — some networks take time to register.
5. Verify device is not set to "home network only."
6. If still failing: Check if destination country is on the unsupported list.

### Unsupported Destinations

Some countries do not have roaming agreements. If a customer travels to an unsupported country:
- Roaming will not work.
- This is NOT a service failure — it is a coverage limitation.
- Agents should inform the customer and cannot offer a resolution.
- Advise the customer to purchase a local SIM in the destination.

### Problem: Unexpectedly high roaming bill

1. Check roaming usage logs.
2. Verify customer was aware of roaming charges (check if roaming was user-enabled or system-enabled).
3. If roaming was enabled without customer's explicit instruction: Escalate.
4. If the charge is accurate and customer was notified: Follow standard billing dispute process (KB-001).

## Escalation Criteria

- Customer's destination country should support roaming but service is unavailable.
- Roaming was enabled without customer consent.
- Roaming charges exceed ₹5,000 unexpectedly.
- Customer is currently abroad and unable to use emergency services.
