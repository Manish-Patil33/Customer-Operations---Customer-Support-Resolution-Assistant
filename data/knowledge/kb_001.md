# KB-001 — Billing Adjustment Policy

**Article ID:** KB-001  
**Category:** Billing  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-08-15

## Summary

This policy governs how billing adjustments, credits, and corrections are handled for all residential and SMB customers. Adjustments may be requested when a billing discrepancy, overcharge, or service disruption causes an incorrect invoice amount.

## When a Billing Adjustment is Applicable

A billing adjustment may be granted in the following scenarios:

1. **Overcharge due to plan mismatch:** Customer was billed for a higher plan tier than the one subscribed.
2. **Pro-rata calculation error:** Plan upgrade or downgrade was applied mid-cycle but billing was not pro-rated correctly.
3. **Service outage credit:** An outage lasting more than 4 hours in a 30-day period entitles the customer to a pro-rated credit for the affected period. Outages must be verified against network maintenance logs.
4. **Duplicate charge:** Same invoice line item charged twice.
5. **Late payment fee waiver (first-time):** Customers with clean payment history (no late payments in the past 12 months) may receive a one-time waiver of late payment fees.

## Procedure

1. **Identify the discrepancy:** Review the current and previous invoice line items. Confirm the plan on record vs. the plan being billed.
2. **Verify the cause:** Check maintenance records for outages; check plan change history for migration dates.
3. **Calculate the adjustment:** Use the pro-rata formula — (Monthly Rate / Days in Month) × Affected Days.
4. **Apply or recommend adjustment:**
   - Adjustments ≤ ₹500 can be applied by a Tier-1 agent.
   - Adjustments between ₹500–₹2,000 require Tier-2 supervisor approval.
   - Adjustments > ₹2,000 require Finance team review and approval.
5. **Inform the customer:** Send a billing adjustment notice with the adjusted amount and reference number.

## Exceptions

- **No adjustment without evidence:** Claims of overcharge must be supported by either account billing history or network outage records. Verbal claims alone are insufficient.
- **Time limit:** Billing adjustment requests must be submitted within **60 days** of the disputed invoice date. Requests outside this window are automatically ineligible (exception: proven system errors).
- **Promotional plan adjustments:** Customers on promotional or bundled plans are subject to different pricing rules. Adjustments for these customers must be escalated to the Commercial team.
- **Post-disconnection credits:** No billing adjustment will be applied to accounts disconnected for non-payment.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to Tier-2 if:
- The billing discrepancy involves a plan migration error spanning more than 3 months.
- The customer disputes an adjustment that was already applied.
- The adjustment exceeds ₹2,000.
- The invoice contains items that cannot be matched to a known product or add-on.
- The customer is on a corporate or enterprise account.
- The customer requests a refund to source (not credit to account).
