# KB-009 — Account Verification Policy

**Article ID:** KB-009  
**Category:** Account  
**Version:** 2.3  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-09-01

## Summary

Identity verification procedures that must be followed before disclosing account details, applying changes, or processing sensitive requests.

## Why Verification is Mandatory

Account verification protects customers from unauthorized access, SIM swaps, and account takeover fraud. Every agent must verify identity before acting on any account.

## Verification Levels

### Level 1 — Basic Verification (Read-Only Access)

Required for: Checking account status, confirming plan, viewing bill amount.

Must verify **2 of the following**:
1. Full name on account.
2. Registered mobile number.
3. Service address (first line).
4. Date of birth.

### Level 2 — Standard Verification (Account Changes)

Required for: Plan changes, billing adjustments, address changes.

Must verify Level 1 PLUS **one** of:
- Last invoice amount (within ₹50).
- Last recharge date (within 3 days).
- Linked email address.

### Level 3 — Enhanced Verification (High-Risk Actions)

Required for: SIM replacement, account transfer, closure, removing security PIN.

Must verify Level 2 PLUS:
- Government-issued ID number (last 4 digits).
- Registered email OTP (sent during session).

**NOTE:** Enhanced verification cannot be completed on inbound calls alone. The customer must also complete an OTP sent to their registered email or mobile number.

## Failed Verification

If a customer fails verification:
1. Do NOT disclose any account information.
2. Offer to send a verification link to the registered email.
3. If the customer claims they do not have access to the registered email: Escalate — the customer must visit a physical store with ID proof.
4. **Do NOT bypass verification** even if the customer sounds distressed or claims an emergency. This is a strict security requirement.

## Exception: Business Accounts

Business accounts have an authorized contacts list. Verify caller against the authorized contact list before proceeding.

## Important: Account Notes

Some accounts have a **Security Note** flag. If a security note is present:
1. Follow the specific instructions in the security note.
2. These are placed by senior agents or the Security team.
3. Standard verification may be insufficient — follow the security note.

## Escalation Criteria

- Customer fails verification after 3 attempts.
- Customer claims identity theft or unauthorized account changes.
- Security note present that requires escalation.
- Business account and caller is not on authorized contacts list.
