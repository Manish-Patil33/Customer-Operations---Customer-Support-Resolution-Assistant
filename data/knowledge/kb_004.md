# KB-004 — Plan Changes (Upgrade & Downgrade)

**Article ID:** KB-004  
**Category:** Plans  
**Version:** 2.5  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Last Updated:** 2024-08-20

## Summary

Procedures for plan upgrades, downgrades, and add-on modifications for broadband and mobile customers.

## Plan Upgrade

### Eligibility
- Account must be in Active status.
- No outstanding balance exceeding ₹500 (unpaid invoices block upgrade).
- Cannot upgrade from a terminated or suspended account.

### Upgrade Process

1. Confirm the customer's current plan and the requested new plan.
2. Verify upgrade is available in the customer's service area (not all plans available in all areas).
3. Inform the customer:
   - **Effective date:** Upgrades take effect within **2 hours** for mobile plans and within **24 hours** for broadband plans (fiber provisioning).
   - **Pro-rata billing:** The difference in plan cost will be billed pro-rata for the remainder of the current billing cycle.
   - **No cancellation fee** applies to upgrades.
4. Apply the plan change in the system.
5. Send confirmation SMS/email.

### Pro-Rata Calculation for Upgrade
Formula: `(New Plan Price - Old Plan Price) / Days in Month × Remaining Days`

Example: Upgrading from ₹499 to ₹899 on Day 20 of a 30-day month:
- Difference: ₹400
- Remaining days: 10
- Pro-rata charge: (₹400 / 30) × 10 = ₹133.33

## Plan Downgrade

### Important Restrictions

**CRITICAL:** Broadband plan downgrades are only effective from the **next billing cycle start date**. A customer cannot downgrade mid-cycle and receive a partial credit for the current cycle.

**Exception:** If the upgrade was applied in error (system error or agent error), the downgrade and refund may be applied immediately with supervisor approval.

### Downgrade Eligibility
- All plans can be downgraded to any lower tier plan.
- Promotional plans: Customers on a promotional plan cannot downgrade within the first 6 months of the promotional period. Attempting to do so triggers early termination and loss of promotional pricing.

### Downgrade Process
1. Verify current plan and requested plan.
2. Inform the customer the downgrade takes effect at next billing cycle.
3. Confirm the customer understands the speed/data reduction.
4. Apply downgrade scheduled for next cycle.
5. Send confirmation.

## Add-ons

Add-ons (extra data packs, speed boosts, international calling bundles) can be added or removed immediately. Pro-rata rules apply.

## Contradictory Scenario

**NOTE FOR SUPPORT AGENTS:** The system may show a "Plan Updated" status while provisioning is still in progress. If a customer reports their speed has not changed 24 hours after a broadband upgrade, verify provisioning status before concluding the upgrade failed. Do NOT tell the customer their upgrade is complete if provisioning is still running.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate if:
- Customer disputes their current plan and the system record does not match their recollection.
- Upgrade request cannot be processed due to system error (not account eligibility).
- Customer on a promotional plan requests downgrade within restricted period and disputes early termination penalty.
- Customer reports plan was changed without their authorization.
