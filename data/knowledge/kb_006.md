# KB-006 — SIM Replacement Procedure

**Article ID:** KB-006  
**Category:** Mobile  
**Version:** 1.9  
**Effective Date:** 2023-05-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-06-15

## Summary

Procedure for handling SIM card replacement requests due to loss, damage, theft, or upgrade to a different SIM format (Standard → Micro → Nano → eSIM).

## Eligibility

SIM replacement is available for:
- Lost SIM (customer must declare the loss; a verification step is required).
- Damaged or malfunctioning SIM.
- Upgrade to Nano SIM or eSIM.
- Device upgrade requiring a different SIM form factor.

**NOT eligible for free SIM replacement:**
- Second replacement within 90 days (₹99 fee applies).
- SIM replacement for a number that was already suspended for non-payment (payment must be cleared first).

## Step 1 — Identity Verification

**MANDATORY:** Before processing any SIM replacement, the agent MUST verify the customer's identity.

Required verification:
1. Registered mobile number (must match account).
2. Date of birth on file.
3. **One** of the following: Last 4 digits of registered ID, last invoice amount (within ₹50), last recharge date.

**SECURITY NOTE:** SIM swaps are a common vector for fraud. If the customer fails identity verification, do NOT proceed. Escalate to the Security team.

## Step 2 — Temporary SIM Lock

Once replacement is initiated:
1. Lock the existing SIM immediately to prevent unauthorized use.
2. Inform the customer the existing SIM will stop working within 30 minutes.

## Step 3 — Replacement Options

| Method | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Walk-in (store) | Same day | Free (first replacement) |
| Home delivery | 24-48 hours | Free (first replacement) |
| eSIM provisioning | Immediate (online) | Free |

## Step 4 — eSIM Provisioning

For eSIM:
1. Verify device supports eSIM.
2. Send QR code to registered email.
3. Customer scans QR code in device settings.
4. Activation takes 5-15 minutes.

**NOTE:** eSIM cannot be issued to accounts with outstanding balance over ₹1,000.

## Escalation Criteria

- Customer fails identity verification.
- Customer reports SIM was swapped without their authorization (possible fraud — immediate Security escalation).
- Multiple SIM replacement requests for same number within 30 days.
- eSIM provisioning fails after 3 attempts.
