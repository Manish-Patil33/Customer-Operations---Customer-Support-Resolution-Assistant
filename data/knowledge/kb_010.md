# KB-010 — Refund Handling Policy

**Article ID:** KB-010  
**Category:** Billing  
**Version:** 1.7  
**Effective Date:** 2023-10-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-07-01

## Summary

Policy governing refunds to the customer's original payment method as distinct from account credits. This article applies only to monetary refunds. For account credits, see KB-001.

## When Refunds Apply

A refund (to original payment method) applies in:
1. Service was not delivered after a confirmed payment (e.g., plan was charged but never activated).
2. Customer was charged after a confirmed account closure (post-cancellation charge).
3. Duplicate payment — same invoice paid twice.
4. Customer opted for a refund instead of account credit (subject to approval).
5. Overpayment above total outstanding balance.

## Refund Timelines

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Credit/Debit Card | 5-7 business days |
| UPI | 3-5 business days |
| Net Banking | 5-7 business days |
| Cash (store) | Physical cheque or NEFT within 10 business days |

**IMPORTANT:** Agents cannot speed up refund timelines. The timeline is determined by the payment network and bank processing, not by the company.

## Process

1. Identify the duplicate/erroneous payment transaction ID.
2. Verify the customer's payment method is still active (expired cards cannot receive refunds — alternate method required).
3. Raise a refund request in the billing system with:
   - Transaction ID
   - Refund amount
   - Reason code
   - Customer communication preference
4. Tier-1 agents can initiate refunds up to ₹500.
5. Refunds > ₹500 require Tier-2 approval.
6. Refunds > ₹5,000 require Finance team processing (timeline may be longer).

## Account Credit vs. Refund

**Key Policy:** If the customer requests a refund and an account credit is offered instead, the agent must clearly explain the difference. The customer has the right to request a refund to their original payment source. However:
- Refunds take longer.
- Some promotional payments (bonus data purchases, discounted recharges) are **not eligible for monetary refunds** — only account credits.

## Exceptions and Edge Cases

- **Gifted plan payments:** If someone else paid for the customer's plan, the refund goes to the payer's payment method, not the account holder.
- **International payments:** Refunds may incur currency conversion fees (not within our control).
- **Closed payment accounts:** If the original payment method is closed, the customer must provide an alternative. This requires Tier-2 handling.

## Escalation Criteria

- Refund amount exceeds ₹500 (requires Tier-2 approval).
- Refund exceeds ₹5,000 (Finance team).
- Customer's original payment method is invalid or closed.
- Customer disputes a refund that was already processed but not received.
- Refund request is outside the 60-day policy window.
