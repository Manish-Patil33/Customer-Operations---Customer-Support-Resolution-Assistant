# KB-014 — Account Closure and Cancellation

**Article ID:** KB-014  
**Category:** Account  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Effective Date:** 2023-12-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-07-15

## Summary

Procedures for handling account closure, service cancellation, and number portability requests.

## Types of Closure

### Voluntary Closure

Customer initiates closure due to:
- Switching providers
- Relocating outside coverage area
- Dissatisfaction with service

### Involuntary Closure

System-initiated closure due to:
- Non-payment (>30 days)
- Fraud determination
- Deceased account holder (estate request)

## Voluntary Closure — Process

### Eligibility Check
1. Verify identity (Level 3 per KB-009).
2. Confirm no outstanding balance. If balance exists, it must be cleared before closure.
3. Check for active contracts: Postpaid plans may have minimum contract periods (usually 12 months). Early cancellation may incur a penalty.

### Early Termination Fee
- **Postpaid with contract:** ₹500 or 1 month's plan cost, whichever is higher.
- **No contract:** No termination fee.
- **During promotional period:** Loss of promotional credits only; no monetary penalty unless contractually specified.

### Number Portability (MNP)
If the customer wants to keep their number and move to another provider:
1. Generate a Unique Porting Code (UPC).
2. UPC is valid for 15 days.
3. **IMPORTANT:** Generating a UPC does NOT close the account. Closure only happens when the new provider confirms port-in success.
4. Do NOT close the account before port-in confirmation — the customer may lose their number.

### Closure Timeline
- Mobile: Effective within 24 hours.
- Broadband: Scheduled for next business day. Equipment return may be required.

## Data Retention After Closure

- Billing data is retained for 7 years (regulatory requirement).
- Personal data is anonymized after 90 days per privacy policy.
- Account records are accessible to the customer for 30 days after closure.

## Critical Edge Case

**CONFLICT:** If a customer claims they cancelled their account but the account shows as active and continues to be billed, this is a **serious billing error.** Do NOT apply a standard billing adjustment. Escalate immediately — this requires a full audit of the closure process.

## Escalation Criteria

- Customer disputes an early termination fee they were not informed about.
- Closure request cannot be processed due to system error.
- Customer is deceased (estate handling — requires documentation).
- Account continues to be billed after closure confirmation.
- Customer requests closure but has an unresolved support ticket that affects their decision.
