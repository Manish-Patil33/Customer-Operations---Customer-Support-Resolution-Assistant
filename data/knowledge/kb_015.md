# KB-015 — Privacy and Data Requests

**Article ID:** KB-015  
**Category:** Account  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective Date:** 2024-04-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-08-01

## Summary

Handling customer requests related to their personal data, including data access requests, data deletion, and data correction under applicable privacy regulations.

## Types of Requests

### Data Access Request (DSAR)
Customer requests a copy of all data held about them.
- Must be fulfilled within **30 days** of verified request.
- Requires Level 3 verification (KB-009).
- Escalate to Privacy team — this cannot be handled by Tier-1.

### Data Correction
Customer requests correction of inaccurate information (name spelling, address, date of birth).
- Name and address changes: Standard account change process (Level 2 verification).
- Date of birth corrections: Require ID proof — escalate to Tier-2.

### Data Deletion
Customer requests deletion of their personal data (Right to Erasure):
- Only applicable to accounts that have been closed for more than 90 days.
- Cannot delete data that is required for legal or billing record purposes (7-year retention applies).
- Escalate to Privacy team.

## Call Recording Disclosure

If a customer asks whether their call is being recorded:
- **Answer: Yes** — all support calls are recorded for quality assurance.
- Customer may request a copy of a specific call recording (subject to data access request process).
- Customer may request that their call NOT be recorded — this overrides the recording only for the current call and only for quality assurance purposes (regulatory recordings cannot be disabled).

## IMPORTANT LIMITATION

This support team does NOT have direct access to delete customer data or fulfill data access requests. All such requests must be routed to the Privacy team through the formal process. Do NOT promise specific timelines beyond the regulatory 30-day requirement.

## Escalation Criteria

- Any DSAR (Data Subject Access Request).
- Data deletion request.
- Customer reports unauthorized disclosure of their data to a third party.
- Any request related to data breach.
